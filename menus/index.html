Weekly programme

What each day of the week holds

The school runs two weekly programmes. Both are reproduced here exactly as they are printed.

Weekly programme A

To confirm: which class this weekly programme belongs to

  1. Monday

    • Introduction of the letter and number of the week.
    • Names — practice writing their names.
    • Patterns.
    • News of the weekend.

  2. Tuesday

    • Cutting and pasting.
    • Story.

  3. Wednesday

    • Names: practice writing names.
    • Opposites, differences and so on.
    • Worksheets.
    • Singing and rhymes.

  4. Thursday

    • Names: practice writing names.
    • Colour and shape.
    • Maths worksheet.
    • Story.

  5. Friday

    • Bakerman.
    • Playdough and similar.

Every day is a new adventure!

Weekly programme B

To confirm: which class this weekly programme belongs to

  1. Monday

    • Introduction to the letter and number of the week.
    • Fun full-body exercises.
    • News of the weekend.

  2. Tuesday

    • Fine motor activity — playdough, threading, cutting.
    • Singing and rhymes.
    • Learning the shape of the week.

  3. Wednesday

    • Practice our letter and number of the week.
    • Story time.
    • Gross motor activities — animal walks, ball games, running games.

  4. Thursday

    • Fine motor activities — cutting skills, tearing.
    • Puzzles.
    • Practice our letter, number, shape and colour of the week.

  5. Friday

    • Bakerman.
    • Dancing and singing.
    • Fun Day Friday.

Let's learn together and have fun together
