Questions

The things parents ask first

Is the enrolment fee refundable?
No. The enrolment fee is a once-off, non-refundable charge, and no application is processed until proof of payment is received.
Do you offer a discount for paying in advance?
Yes. Paying per term — four payments in advance — carries a 5% discount, and paying annually in advance carries 10%.
Is there a sibling discount?
Yes. The first child pays the full rates and a sibling discount of R300 applies.
What happens if I collect late?
A late collection fee of R70 applies for every 10 minutes after 17:30.

Ready to enrol?

Start with a visit. If it feels right, the enrolment form takes about ten minutes.
