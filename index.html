Welcome

A fun and professional child day care facility

It is said: 'If you live your passion you never work a day in your life!' — that is how the dream of Little Caterpillars emerged and became the reality it is today! Little Caterpillars is a fun and professional child day care facility run by a dynamic team who are as passionate about giving your child(ren) the best early education possible as you, the parents, are.

  • 3 months – 6 years

    From the Baby Centre through to the reception year, on one campus.

  • Five classes

    Butterfly, Dragonfly, Ladybug, Caterpillar and Busy Bees.

  • Midrand
