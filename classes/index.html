3 – 12 months

Butterfly Class

Baby Centre

Our Baby Centre takes babies from three months. The room is capped at ten babies and staffed by a qualified teacher with an assistant, so there is always a familiar pair of hands. Every baby goes home with a communication book recording nappy changes, feeds and naps — so you know how the day went before you have even put the bags down.

  • A maximum of 10 babies in the Baby Centre.
  • A qualified teacher and an assistant.
  • A daily communication book covering nappy changes, feeds and naps.

1 – 2 years

Dragonfly Class

The Dragonflies are on the move. This year is about gross motor development through play — the walking, running and stopping milestones that come thick and fast between one and two.

  • Gross motor focus, through play.
  • Walking, running and stopping milestones.

Ladybug Class

3 – 4 years

Caterpillar Class

The Caterpillars work to a weekly theme. Across the week the syllabus covers fine motor and gross motor work, cognitive development, language, movement, hand and finger skills, and the emotional and social side of being three.

  • A detailed weekly-theme syllabus.
  • Fine motor, gross motor and cognitive development.
  • Language, movement, hand and finger skills, emotional and social growth.

4 – 6 years

Busy Bees Class

Reception year

The reception year, aimed squarely at Grade 1 readiness. Literacy, life skills and numeracy sit alongside gross motor and kinaesthetic work, music, art and science.

  • Grade 1 readiness.
  • Literacy, life skills and numeracy.
  • Gross motor and kinaesthetics, music, art and science.
